# Welcome

Welcome to your Research Briefings feed. This is a short test episode to confirm that everything works.

Here is how it works. You give Claude a topic. Claude searches the web, reads the best sources, and writes a script of about eight minutes, designed to be listened to rather than read. A free neural voice then turns that script into an MP3, which is added to this private podcast feed.

Because it is a podcast feed, new episodes show up automatically on your phone. On Alexa, you can ask a podcast skill to play the latest episode.

That's it. Next time, you will hear a real briefing on a topic of your choice.
